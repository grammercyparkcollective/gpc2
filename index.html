Grammercy Park
Neighborhood Collective

No fees, no dues, just solidarity.

We're a group of neighbors working to build a more connected, more engaged community in Omaha. Plant swaps, block parties, clean ups, housing advocacy: we go where the community leads. We primarily serve neighbors in Grammercy Park, located between Benson and Dundee (east of Country Club), but anyone is welcome.

What affects one, affects all.

What We Do

Projects & Advocacy

We work with neighbors and the city on issues that shape everyday life here: everything from broken streetlights to feeding our neighbors (and community cats), supporting the small businesses that make this a place worth living, and advocating for better policies that govern our community.

  • Block parties, plant swaps, and events that build real community
  • Showing up for the local businesses that anchor the neighborhood
  • Affordable housing policies and protections for long-term residents
  • Community gardens and shared green spaces
  • We're tracking TIF development projects in our neighborhood closely

A few of the places that make this neighborhood what it is: Global Foods, Green Line Cafe, Simpson's Brakes, Innovative Roofing, Sgt. Peffer's, the Homy Inn, and PKP Screenprinting. Spend your money here when you can.

Curious about a project on your block?

New developments, construction, and road closures are reshaping the neighborhood, and it isn't always obvious what's planned or who to ask. That's something we can help with. Get in touch and we'll share what we've found, point you toward the right person at the city, and keep you posted as things move.

Omaha TIF Tracker

Tax Increment Financing refunds increased property taxes from new development back to developers instead of to schools, parks, and public services. An unintended consequence: homeowners near TIF projects often see their own taxes rise and are gentrified out of our own neighborhoods.

Get Involved

Join Us

No fees. No dues. No membership to buy. What we do runs on neighbors giving a few hours when they can. Spread the word on your block, pitch in a skill, or just show up.

If you'd rather support us with money than hours, that's welcome too, and it's always free will, pay what you want. It covers the real costs of doing this: printing flyers, supplies for events, mailing things to people who aren't online. Nothing we do is ever gated behind giving, and plenty of the people who show up the most have never given a cent. Get in touch if you'd like to chip in.

One form, and you're in

It takes a couple of minutes and asks how you'd like to hear from us. It's also where you tell us if you want to be a Block Connector, help plan events, or join our email newsletter.

Join the Collective →

What a Block Connector does

GPC is non-hierarchical: we go where the community leads us. Block Connectors are how we make that real. You'd be our point person on your block. We send you updates, resources, and things worth sharing, and you get them to your neighbors however works best for you. A text thread, a flyer on a door, a conversation over the fence.

No meetings required. Just a neighbor who gives a damn. Say yes to it on the form above.

Just moved here? It's the fastest way to meet the people on your block.

Not ready to join? Tell us what you think instead

Our community feedback survey asks what's working on your block, what you'd change, and what you'd say to the Mayor if you had the chance. Responses are anonymous. We ask for an email only to keep out spam, and it's never attached to your answers or shared. Set aside ten minutes or so; there's a lot of room to write.
